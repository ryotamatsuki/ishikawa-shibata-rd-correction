Consistent with the IJIO author-facing submission route observed on 31 August 2026, the manuscript should be entered under the `Research Paper` article type; its title and this letter identify its editorial function explicitly as a Comment.

The note concerns Nana Ishikawa and Takashi Shibata (2021), “R&D competition and cooperation with asymmetric spillovers in an oligopoly market,” International Review of Economics & Finance 72, 624–642 (doi:10.1016/j.iref.2020.12.016). The manuscript does not challenge the published equilibrium solutions. It corrects threshold characterizations derived from those solutions.

The note establishes three tightly bounded results. First, an exact regular implicit-function calculation shows that the individual competition–cooperation boundary is not invariant to the R&D-cost parameter in general, while an exact symmetric cancellation explains the special cost-parameter-independent crossing. Second, an analytic factorization shows that the aggregate equality boundary is not generally a constant-sum line in the two spillover parameters, and a separate regular-root calculation shows local dependence on the R&D-cost parameter. Third, an exact admissible counterexample, combined with continuity, shows that the published fixed cutoff and the retained equilibrium comparison disagree on an open parameter set.

The subject is directly within theoretical industrial organization: the correction concerns oligopoly, R&D competition and cooperation, asymmetric spillovers, and the geometry of regime boundaries. The manuscript is deliberately short and does not add a new model, welfare analysis, or empirical claim. It also states explicitly which results of the original article remain valid.

The proposition-level calculations are supported by deterministic Python/SymPy verification code and a reviewer-safe reproducibility package. No external empirical dataset is used. The manuscript includes the generative-AI disclosure required by current Elsevier policy.
