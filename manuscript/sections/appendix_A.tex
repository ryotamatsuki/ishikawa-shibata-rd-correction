\section{Cleared individual equality polynomial}
For $\lambda=0$, $\beta_2=0$, $B=\beta_1$, and $G=\gamma$, the regular equality $D_1=0$ is equivalent to
\begin{align*}
Q_I(B,G)=&-27(2B-1)G^2
 +18(2B^3-7B^2+12B-5)G\\
&-4(10B^3-35B^2+45B-18)=0.
\end{align*}
At $(B,G)=(1/3,44/27)$, the denominator cleared to obtain this equation equals $808640/19683$. The exact derivative values used in Proposition \ref{prop:individual} are $Q_{I,G}=-4/3$ and $Q_{I,B}=-236/27$.
