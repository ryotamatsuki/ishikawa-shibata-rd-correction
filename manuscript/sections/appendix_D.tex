\section{Exact regularity certificate for Proposition 3}
At the parameter point of Proposition \ref{prop:misclass}, the firm-level R\&D choices are
\[
 x^n=\left(\frac{110500}{100239},\frac{83220}{33413}\right),\qquad
 x^c=\left(\frac{44500}{19119},\frac{8300}{6373}\right).
\]
Outputs and marginal costs are strictly positive. For the noncooperative first-stage problem, the own second derivatives are
\[
-\frac{12211}{1250},\qquad -\frac{82}{9},
\]
and the FOC-Jacobian determinant is $33413/375>0$. For the cooperative problem, the Hessian diagonal entries are
\[
-\frac{2391}{250},\qquad -\frac{80}{9},
\]
with determinant $6373/75>0$. The relevant closed-form denominators are nonzero. These strict inequalities provide the local regularity used in the continuity argument.
