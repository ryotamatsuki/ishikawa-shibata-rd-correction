\section{Aggregate factorization and local root}
At $\lambda=0$, transform to $s=\beta_1+\beta_2$ and $d=\beta_1-\beta_2$. On $s=1$, the cleared numerator is $d^2(18\gamma+d^2-9)/2$. For the separate local-$\gamma$ argument at $\beta_2=0$, the general cleared aggregate polynomial is
\begin{align*}
Q_A(B,G)=&20B^4+18B^3G-106B^3-108B^2G+216B^2\\
&-27BG^2+171BG-198B+27G^2-90G+72.
\end{align*}
At $G=2$ it factors as $2B(10B^3-35B^2+18)$. The cubic is strictly decreasing on $(0,1)$ and changes sign between $4097/5000$ and $1639/2000$, which isolates the unique nonzero root used in Proposition \ref{prop:aggregate}.
