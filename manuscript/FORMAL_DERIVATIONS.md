## Maintained scope

Use the published noncooperative and cooperative R&D closed forms retained in Stage 0. Define

- `D_i = x_i^n - x_i^c`,
- `D_A = (x_1^n+x_2^n) - (x_1^c+x_2^c)`.

All equalities below are on regular parameter regions where the retained closed forms are defined. No welfare-ranking claim is made.

## Proposition 1 — individual boundary

### 1. Exact asymmetric slice

Set `lambda=0` and `beta_2=0`, and write `B=beta_1`, `G=gamma`. After clearing nonzero denominators,

`D_1=0` is equivalent to `Q_I(B,G)=0`, where

`Q_I = -27(2B-1)G^2 + 18(2B^3-7B^2+12B-5)G - 4(10B^3-35B^2+45B-18)`.

The exact denominator is

`[10B^2G-16BG-9G^2+20G-4] [6B^2G-24BG+8B-27G^2+48G-16]`.

At the asymmetric admissible point

`B=1/3, beta_2=0, lambda=0, G=44/27`,

we have exactly

- `Q_I=0`,
- `partial Q_I/partial G = -4/3`,
- `partial Q_I/partial B = -236/27`,
- denominator `=808640/19683 != 0`.

Therefore the implicit-function theorem applies and

`dB*/dG = -(Q_G/Q_B) = -9/59 != 0`.

This exact regular point is sufficient to reject a globally gamma-invariant threshold characterization.

Admissibility is not a corner artifact. At `a=100,c=50`:

- `x^n=(150/19,540/19)`,
- `x^c=(150/19,1800/133)`,
- `q^n=(220/19,660/19)`,
- `q^c=(2200/133,3300/133)`,
- marginal costs are positive in both regimes.

The noncooperative own second derivatives are `-82/81` and `-20/27`, with FOC-Jacobian determinant `1520/2187>0`. The cooperative Hessian has diagonal entries `-80/81` and `-14/27`, with determinant `532/2187>0`.

### 2. Exact symmetric cancellation

On `beta_1=beta_2=b`, the numerator of `D_1` factors as

`-gamma(a-c)(lambda-3)^2 [ b(lambda^2-3lambda+4) + lambda^2-lambda-2 ]`.

On the regular domain, equality therefore gives

`b*(lambda) = (2+lambda-lambda^2)/(lambda^2-3lambda+4)`.

The R&D-cost parameter cancels exactly on this symmetric slice. This explains the special gamma-independent crossing without implying generic invariance.

## Proposition 2 — aggregate boundary

### 1. Published straight line fails under asymmetry

Set `lambda=0`, and introduce

`s=beta_1+beta_2`, `d=beta_1-beta_2`.

After clearing denominators, let the aggregate equality polynomial be `Q_A=0`. On the published line `s=1`, exact substitution yields

`Q_A |_(s=1) = d^2(18 gamma + d^2 - 9)/2`.

For the maintained `gamma>=1`, the factor `18 gamma + d^2 - 9` is strictly positive. After cancellation of this nonzero common factor, the aggregate difference on the published line is exactly

`D_A |_(lambda=0,s=1) = 32 gamma d^2(a-c) / {[-6 gamma+d^2+3][36 gamma^2-20 gamma d^2-36 gamma+d^4+6d^2+9]}`.

Hence, wherever the retained closed forms are regular, every asymmetric point `d!=0` on the published line fails aggregate equality. The symmetric point is the special intersection; the straight line is not the general asymmetric equality locus.

### 2. Exact local gamma dependence of the corrected aggregate locus

Set `lambda=0`, `beta_2=0`, and denote `B=beta_1`. After clearing denominators,

`D_A=0` is equivalent to `Q_A(B,G)=0`, with

`Q_A = 20B^4 +18B^3G -106B^3 -108B^2G +216B^2 -27BG^2 +171BG -198B +27G^2 -90G +72`.

At `G=2`,

`Q_A(B,2)=2B H(B)`, where `H(B)=10B^3-35B^2+18`.

Since

`H'(B)=10B(3B-7)<0` for `0<B<1`,

and

- `H(4097/5000)=25163173/12500000000>0`,
- `H(1639/2000)=-1366881/800000000<0`,

there is a unique root `alpha` in

`4097/5000 < alpha < 1639/2000`,

so `alpha≈0.81945409098`.

The closed-form denominator at `G=2` is

`16B(5B-8)(3B^2-10B-7)`,

which is nonzero for `0<B<1`.

Using `H(alpha)=0`, exact polynomial reduction gives

`Q_G(alpha,2)=-(9/5)(25alpha^2-35alpha+8)`

and

`Q_B(alpha,2)=2(35alpha^2-54)`.

On the isolating interval, `25alpha^2-35alpha+8<0`, while `35alpha^2-54<0`. Therefore

`Q_G(alpha,2)>0`, `Q_B(alpha,2)<0`,

and the implicit-function theorem yields

`d alpha*/dG = -Q_G/Q_B > 0`.

Thus the corrected aggregate equality locus is locally gamma-dependent at a regular asymmetric interior point.

At `G=2`, `beta_2=0`, the equilibrium expressions reduce to

- `x_1^n=50(B-2)/(3B^2-10B-7)`,
- `x_2^n=100(B^2-3B-1)/(3B^2-10B-7)`,
- `x_1^c=x_2^c=-50/(5B-8)`.

For `0<B<1`, and hence at `B=alpha`, R&D levels, outputs and marginal costs are positive and the relevant first-stage curvature determinants have the required signs. The root is therefore not generated by a singular or corner artifact.

## Proposition 3 — exact positive-measure misclassification

At

`a=100, c=50, gamma=10, lambda=0, beta_1=49/50, beta_2=0`,

the published rule uses average spillover `49/100<1/2` and therefore places the point on the competitive-R&D side of the fixed cutoff.

The retained closed forms instead give exactly

- `x^n=(110500/100239, 83220/33413)`,
- `x^c=(44500/19119, 8300/6373)`.

Thus

- aggregate noncooperative R&D `=360160/100239 = 3.59301269964784...`,
- aggregate cooperative R&D `=69400/19119 = 3.62989696113813...`,
- `D_A = -23562520/638823147 = -0.0368842614902932... < 0`.

The sign reversal is exact, not a floating-point artifact.

At the same point:

- `q^n=(1625000/100239,624150/33413)`,
- `q^c=(325480/19119,349490/19119)`,
- marginal costs under noncooperation are `(4901450/100239,4654000/100239)`,
- marginal costs under cooperation are `(911450/19119,887440/19119)`.

All are positive. Noncooperative own second derivatives are `-12211/1250` and `-82/9`, with FOC-Jacobian determinant `33413/375>0`. The cooperative Hessian has diagonal entries `-2391/250` and `-80/9`, with determinant `6373/75>0`.

The retained rational closed-form denominators are nonzero at the point. Therefore the equilibrium functions are continuous on some neighborhood. The published cutoff inequality is also strict (`0.49<0.5`) and the corrected aggregate inequality is strict (`D_A<0`). Both signs persist on a sufficiently small open neighborhood. Hence the published and corrected classifications disagree on an open set and therefore on a set of positive Lebesgue measure.
