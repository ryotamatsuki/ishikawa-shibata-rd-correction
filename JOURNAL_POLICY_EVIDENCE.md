## Cross-publisher conclusions

1. **IJIO has the strongest current precedent:** a January 2026 comment correcting a theoretical condition in an earlier IJIO paper.
2. **IREF has genuine third-party comment precedent but a current Gold-OA cost disadvantage.** Author corrigenda are a separate route and are not the model for this project.
3. **Economics Letters is explicitly unsuitable for comments and normally limited to 2,000 words.**
4. **RIO and Journal of Economics provide verified no-APC subscription routes.**
5. Current exact submission-portal labels, review anonymization and IJIO submission-fee status must be checked in Stage 5 wherever the public journal guide did not expose them. These are administrative confirmations; no journal-strategy conclusion rests on guessing them.
